# BTB Notification Sound Sources

The Android notification sounds are short, mono PCM WAV derivatives of CC0
recordings from Freesound.

## Super goal / stadium

- App resource: `btb_super_goal.wav`
- Source: `Crowd Cheer` by FoolBoyMedia
- Source page: https://freesound.org/people/FoolBoyMedia/sounds/397434/
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- Processing: a 1.05-second crowd-cheer segment was selected, converted to
  44.1 kHz mono PCM, normalized, filtered, and faded for notification use.

## General referee whistle

- App resource: `btb_referee_whistle.wav`
- Source: `Referee whistle sound.wav` by Rosa-Orenes256
- Source page: https://freesound.org/people/Rosa-Orenes256/sounds/538422/
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- Processing: converted to 44.1 kHz mono PCM, normalized, filtered, and faded
  for a balanced notification level.
