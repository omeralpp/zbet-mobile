## Implementation evidence

### Live switch and pinned matches

- The visible control surface contains one `Canlı` switch plus the independent
  star filter. The switch selects the existing Live or Fixture sibling page;
  Scout source, page, binocular controls and storage draft were removed.
- A pin control uses checkbox semantics and a filled selected state. Its nested
  press stops propagation and never opens match detail.
- Pinned keys live in a versioned, bounded device-local set. They are reconciled
  only against a successful current feed. A non-empty top `Sabitlenenler`
  section preserves feed order; unpin returns the match to its natural kickoff
  group. There is no history, BFF/SAP write, model or notification effect.
- Real-API emulator smoke pinned Bologna–Lazio, moved it to the top group,
  unpinned it and restored `Bugün · 19:30` as its natural group.

### Jinx sleep, wake and daily mood

- The tested motion resolver now owns `SLEEPING` and `REACTING` alongside
  ambient, drag, menu, guide and suspended states. Thirty seconds of eligible
  inactivity selects the closed-eye frame, stops hover/sparkle/micro-expression
  work and leaves only a 6.4-second restrained breath plus a small `zZ` cue.
- The root gesture surface observes `onTouchStart` without becoming the
  responder. The first touch wakes Jinx with a short spring reaction and still
  reaches the intended switch, button, pager or scroll gesture.
- Background, reduced-motion, drag, menu, guide and discovery ownership is
  explicit. A network update does not wake a sleeping Jinx.
- Mood uses the existing React Query `superKpis` cache and the same persisted
  Super star filter as the dashboard. A pure policy derives positive, negative,
  even, empty or unknown from the exact metric day and selected bucket; there is
  no fallback profit calculation and no new polling.
- Positive, negative, even and empty states have compact persistent accessories,
  a bounded anticipation/spring/follow-through/settle sketch and deterministic
  non-repeating factual quips. Humour targets Jinx's own luck, never the user,
  and contains no prediction or betting advice. A day/filter/mood signature
  prevents a same-sign refetch from replaying the full sketch.
- Emulator smoke showed the real empty-day clock accessory and `HESAP BEKLİYOR`
  bubble. A 33-second idle capture showed closed eyes and `zZ`; the first touch
  woke Jinx and changed the Live switch in the same gesture.

### Absolute pressure and minute progress

- One `formatAbsolute` helper now formats the visible pressure difference in the
  live card, its screen-reader sentence, both match/Super `PressureBalance`
  surfaces and the Super decision field metric. Negative raw data is not
  rewritten: the existing home/away derivation and icon/text direction remain
  authoritative.
- A pure minute policy maps Live/Half-time/Finished to a 90-minute bounded ratio,
  preserves the actual 90+ label and hides the surface for `NOT_STARTED`.
- The card presentation is intentionally minimal: a three-pixel line and one
  right-aligned minute label. The container exposes `progressbar`, min/max/now
  and text accessibility values.
- Real-API emulator smoke showed `76'` and `50'` progress lines. Away pressure
  appeared as `2,6` with the airplane icon, and home pressure as `2,7` with the
  home icon; no sign was displayed.
